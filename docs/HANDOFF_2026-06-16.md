## Metric / behaviour changes to be aware of

- **ESOP cards were redefined** to match the sheet: `Vested` = shares of
  instruments with no vesting schedule (was: exercised shares); `Vesting` =
  scheduled instruments' full shares (was: granted − exercised); `Total
  Vesting to date` = scheduled vested-so-far. The `EsopPool` API/type fields
  are now `{ grandTotal, vesting, vested, vestedToDate }`. **Prod ESOP numbers
  will shift** when this merges to `main`.
- The **notification bell is a server read-model** — no notification table.
  Survey nudges are computed from published, non-anonymous, in-window forms
  the user is targeted by and hasn't answered.
